# ALTER DBA ADD AGENT: Practical AI for Database Professionals

Working files for the **ALTER DBA ADD AGENT** blog series on
[sqlserverscience.com](https://www.sqlserverscience.com/).  The series helps
traditional SQL Server DBAs understand how AI coding agents (primarily GitHub
Copilot CLI, with Claude Code referenced where relevant) can help with
day-to-day work.

Series landing page:
[sqlserverscience.com/ai-for-dbas/alter-dba-add-agent/](https://www.sqlserverscience.com/ai-for-dbas/alter-dba-add-agent/)

## What's here

This repository is the source-materials companion to the series.  It contains
the Markdown drafts, the series plan, and the editorial process records, so
readers can inspect how the series was researched, drafted, and revised.

- `drafts/post-00-series-index.md` … `post-18-meta.md` - the 19 main series
  posts, in order.
- `drafts/sat-*.md` - the satellite deep-dive posts that accompany the main
  series with step-by-step walkthroughs for specific scenarios.
- `plan.md` - the series outline, post index, and editorial guidelines.
- `process/log.md` - a chronological log of planning, early iterations, and
  editorial decisions.  It is not a complete transcript of every session, but
  it records the key decisions, critiques, and revision patterns.
- `process/decisions.md` - the rationale behind naming, tone rules, and the
  major editorial choices.

## How this content was produced

The series was coauthored with an AI coding agent and reviewed by a human DBA.
The disclosure, and a discussion of what that does and does not demonstrate, is
in the final post, [`drafts/post-18-meta.md`](drafts/post-18-meta.md).  If a
claim in the series does not match your experience, question it, just as you
would with any technical blog post.
